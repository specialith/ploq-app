# Code of Conduct

Ploq follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't welcome anywhere in the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
**Security > Report a vulnerability** form (marked "conduct"), or by replying to any email from
Ploq. Reports are kept confidential.
